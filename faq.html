Can this actually look like a normal dining table?

Absolutely! That's our entire design superpower. All tech mechanics are fully hidden — it looks like a sleek modern furniture piece 100% of the time.

Is the automatic tech noisy?

Nope. We engineered ultra-quiet precision shuffling tech specifically for home living spaces — no loud buzzing, no disruptive noise.

Is setup complicated?

Zero complexity. Plug-and-play smart power design — no assembly headaches, no confusing tech.

Does it fit standard mahjong tiles?

Yes! Universal sizing built to work with all standard tile sets.

Do you offer warranty on the smart components?

We include a 2-year full warranty on all mechanical & tech systems, plus ongoing friendly support.

Can I design a totally custom table?

Absolutely! Our Atelier series is fully bespoke — design your dream table with our team.

Where do you deliver?

We offer fast, insured nationwide delivery for every modern home and space.
