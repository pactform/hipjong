OUR STORY

We asked: Why can't
mahjong feel modern?

Mahjong is no longer an old-school pastime. It's the go-to chill social night, crew hangout, weekend ritual for millions of modern people. But the furniture? It never updated.

We started Hipjong because we were tired of the trade-off:
Style vs. Function. Pretty vs. Playable. Home vibe vs. Game night setup.

We believe modern gatherings deserve modern furniture.
Sleek design. Smart hidden tech. Zero compromise.

Mahjong isn't just a game anymore.
It's a lifestyle — and your table should match that energy.
